The Problem We Can No Longer Ignore

  • Large amounts of good food are wasted after events and celebrations.
  • Many individuals and families still struggle with hunger every day.
  • There is no quick, organized way to connect surplus food with those in need.
  • Good intentions often fail due to lack of coordination.
  • Food waste harms not just people, but also the environment.

How FeedTheNeed Helps

  • Connects food donors with nearby NGOs and volunteers.
  • Provides a simple platform to share surplus food quickly.
  • Reduces food waste through timely pickup coordination.
  • Supports hunger relief efforts at the community level.
  • Encourages responsible, organized acts of kindness.

How FeedTheNeed Works

A simple, responsible process that turns surplus food into meaningful support — quickly and safely.

Step 1 — You Inform Us – Share details about your surplus food through our simple form.

Step 2 — We Connect – We match your donation with nearby NGOs or volunteers.

Step 3 — Food is Collected – Our team coordinates safe and timely

Step 4 — It Reaches the Needy – The food is delivered to those who need it most.

Food Safety First- FeedTheNeed encourages donation of freshly prepared, untouched surplus food only. All partner NGOs and volunteers are advised to follow safe food handling and timely distribution practices. We promote responsible giving — because care matters as much as intention.

Small actions, when organized with care, can create meaningful change — one shared meal at a time.

Donate Surplus Food

Your extra food can become someone’s next meal. Share it — don’t waste it.

If you have surplus food from a wedding, birthday, event, or gathering, you can make a real difference by donating it. FeedTheNeed helps connect your available food with NGOs and volunteers who can quickly collect and distribute it to people in need. The process is simple, quick, and guided — and every contribution matters.

Quick Guidelines Before Donating:

What You Can Donate

  • Freshly prepared surplus food
  • Properly stored and covered items
  • Vegetarian or non-vegetarian food
  • Packed or bulk food from events

What You Cannot Donate

  • Stale or spoiled food
  • Half-eaten or served plates
  • Food left uncovered for long hours
  • Items past safe consumption time

About Us

FeedTheNeed was born from a simple yet powerful realization — while tons of food are wasted every day, millions of people sleep hungry. As students, we felt that waiting for change was not an option. So we decided to become a part of it.

We are a youth-driven initiative committed to reducing food waste and connecting surplus food with communities that truly need it. Our goal is to build a bridge between donors — individuals, restaurants, and event organizers — and trusted NGOs that distribute food responsibly.

At FeedTheNeed, we believe that every meal matters. A small act of sharing can create a big impact. By coordinating safe food collection and distribution, we aim to turn compassion into action and responsibility into results.

This is more than just a project for us — it is a mission to create awareness, encourage community participation, and ensure that no good food goes to waste while someone nearby is hungry.

Together, we can transform surplus into support and generosity into hope.
